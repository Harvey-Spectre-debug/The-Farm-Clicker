# Farm Clicker 🌾

A small farming clicker game I made for Hack Club Terra. You click to earn coins, buy crops that earn coins for you, and build up your own floating island.

**Play it here:** https://harvey-spectre-debug.github.io/The-Farm-Clicker/

## How to play
1. Press **Click to farm** to earn coins.
2. Buy crops and tomatoes. Each one sits on a plot of your island and earns coins every second.
3. Buy click upgrades so every click gives more coins.
4. Unlock more plots when your island gets full.
5. Buy decorations (tree, cottage, fountain, windmill, castle). Each one gives a permanent income bonus.
6. Own everything? Start a new season. You get a Golden Seed (+25% income) and the world changes: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter.

Your progress saves automatically in your browser.

## Built with
HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No libraries.
